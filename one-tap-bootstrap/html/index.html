Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book. Click a question to open or close its answer.

Open the Contact page, fill in the booking form with your preferred zone and visit time, and send the request. We confirm within the hour during staffed shifts.

OneTap is open 24/7 at Astana, Mangilik El 55/1 — day, night and tournament weekends.

Daytime is a session from 12:00 to 18:00. Night Pass covers the late hours from 23:00 to 07:00. Both presets are available in the booking form and you can adjust the times.

Main Zone suits everyday sessions, VIP Zone offers private booths, Bootcamp Zone is for players who train seriously, and the Tournament Room hosts the weekly brackets.
