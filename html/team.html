How OneTap started

OneTap opened as a two-room LAN spot for a small group of regulars who wanted somewhere better than a cybercafe to play after class. It has since grown into a full club with a Main Zone, a VIP zone, a Bootcamp zone, and a standing tournament room — but the goal hasn't changed: give players a fast, well-kept place to show up and compete.
